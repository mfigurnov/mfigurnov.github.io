Michael Figurnov

Senior Staff Research Scientist · Google DeepMind

I work on pretraining of Gemini models.

Previously, I was a core machine learning researcher behind AlphaFold 2, with contributions to its architecture, training, and predictive accuracy. My earlier research focused on probabilistic machine learning, gradient estimation, and adaptive computation.

Co-author of AlphaFold 3. Research on protein modelling and LLMs for biology.

2019–2021

AlphaFold 2

I designed the Evoformer architecture, co-developed the recycling mechanism, and improved model training and predictive accuracy. I also trained the models used in AlphaFold 2’s winning CASP14 submission.

My architectural contribution was highlighted in John Jumper’s Nobel lecture.
