{\raggedright
I am a Senior Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind in London, working on
pretraining of Gemini models. Previously, I was a core machine learning researcher
behind AlphaFold 2, with contributions to its architecture, training, and predictive
accuracy. My earlier research focused on probabilistic machine learning, gradient
estimation, and adaptive computation.\par}
